FAQ

Fair questions.

Do agents write all the code?

No. Agents draft; I supervise and review every diff through an adversarial pre-ship gate. Upstream maintainers have merged 70+ of my PRs — agent-assisted, human-accountable.

What does agent orchestration actually mean?

Running many agent sessions as one system: spawn, supervise, steer, review, recover — in AO, with a human on the hook for everything that merges.

Can I see the work?

Yes. Everything linked is public: upstream PR lists, gbrain, the oss-site catalog. Private pieces are marked honestly, never dead-linked.

What kind of team is this for?

Teams building AI-native products or developer tooling that want shipping speed without giving up review rigor.

How do you prefer to be reached?

Email, one click from every page. Issues and PRs on my repos reach me too.

Was this site built by agents?
