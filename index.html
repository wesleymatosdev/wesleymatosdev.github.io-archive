CASE STUDIES

Three problems. Three systems shipped.

Warner Bros. Discovery

SENIOR SOFTWARE ENGINEER → STAFF CLOUD/SRE · SEPT 2022 – JUL 2025

$5M/YR SAVED
PROBLEM
A legacy third-party CMS — the Accenture Media Engage predecessor — sat under Warner Bros. Discovery's streaming properties, costing $5 million a year in licensing and infrastructure.
WHAT I BUILT
Led the project that replaced it with a Strapi, React.js and Node.js platform. Created the BFF that powered migrations from legacy codebases to GraphQL, built real-time Kafka and SQS pipelines streaming content from HBO, TNT and DC, and led remediation of more than 100 security vulnerabilities across 50+ repositories.
OUTCOME
The replacement eliminated $5 million a year. The GraphQL APIs behind it handle 15–25M requests a day, and test coverage on the hbo.com and dc.com GraphQL codebases went from 20% to 80%.

2025 · Cloud/SRE CoE: multi-region, multi-cloud CDNs — AWS, Fastly, GCP, Akamai — serving hundreds of millions of users toward a 99.99999% availability target, 95M on HBO alone, behind hbomax.com, cnn.com and discoveryplus.com.

Luxury Presence

SENIOR SOFTWARE ENGINEER II · AUG – OCT 2025

10K+ LINES DEDUPED
PROBLEM
Six NestJS microservices carried more than 10,000 lines of duplicated logic, and integration tests had no way to run against live infrastructure.
WHAT I BUILT
An SDK estimated to replace the duplicated logic across all six microservices, and Testcontainers so integration and E2E tests run in parallel with live PostgreSQL, SQS and Redis.
OUTCOME
More than 10,000 lines of duplication retired across six NestJS microservices, and over 100 test cases now verify core business logic against real dependencies.

Influencer

STAFF SOFTWARE ENGINEER · JAN – JUL 2026

97% LATENCY CUT
PROBLEM
Flaky and slow. Third-party API integrations took up to 30 seconds, and the architecture was holding the product back.
WHAT I BUILT
Rebuilt the whole thing from the ground up: a creator-discovery platform combining AI inference with creator, audience and demographic filters, vector search over AI-generated embeddings, and integrations with TikTok One, Meta and Instagram APIs.
OUTCOME
Third-party API integration latency cut from 30 seconds to 900ms — a 97% reduction. Most of the old flakiness is gone. The platform is harder to break into, too.

Eight years in. The scale changed every year — the standard never did.
